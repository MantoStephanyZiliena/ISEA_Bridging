## Total Quantity Consumes
In this section I will be giving the break down the total consumes needed for the printers when the given time period is 5 years.
1. Inkjet Printer- Canon PIXMA TS8870
   
  Total pages over 5 years:
  750 pages/week × 52 weeks/year × 5 years = 195,000 pages
   
  Split (50% color / 50% black):
   * Black-only pages: 97,500
   * Color pages: 97,500

  Black ink cartridges	97,500 ÷ 400 pages/cartridge = 244 of black ink needed for 5 years

  Color ink cartridges	97,500 ÷ 500 pages/cartridge = 195 of color ink needed

2. Laser Printer- Canon imageCLASS LBP122dw II

  Total pages over 5 years:
  750 pages/week × 52 weeks/year × 5 years = 195,000 pages

  Toner cartridges (071H)	195,000 ÷ 2,500 pages/cartridge = 78 total of toner to be consumed
